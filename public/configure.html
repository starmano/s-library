What's new

Connect your Stremio account

Folders are saved in your own Stremio library, so they follow you to every device.

Your password is passed on to Stremio to sign in and is never stored.

Signed up with Facebook, Google or Apple?

Connect with your auth key instead. Open Stremio Web while signed in, open the browser console and run JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem("profile")).auth.key
